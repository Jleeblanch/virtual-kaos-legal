Privacy Policy

How the Virtual Kaos Pin Publisher handles data

This page covers one specific thing: the automation that connects our Etsy shop to our own Pinterest business account. It does not cover Etsy's or Pinterest's own platforms — see their respective privacy policies for that.

What this is

Virtual Kaos is an independently operated print-on-demand shop on Etsy. The "Virtual Kaos Pin Publisher" is a private, single-account integration we built to automatically publish pins to our own Pinterest business account, linking to our own product listings. It is not a public app, and no one outside Virtual Kaos uses it or authenticates through it.

What we access

  • Our own Pinterest business account's API access token, obtained through Pinterest's standard OAuth authorization.
  • Our own product images, titles, and descriptions — the same content already public on our Etsy shop — used to create pins.
  • Basic performance metrics (impressions, saves, clicks) for pins we've published, if and when we enable Pinterest's reporting features, so we can see what's working.

We do not access, read, collect, or store data belonging to any other Pinterest user, board, or account. This integration only ever acts on our own account, and only to publish content — it does not browse, analyze, or interact with anyone else's Pins or Boards.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We don't collect personal information from Pinterest users, Pinterest visitors, or Etsy customers through this integration.
  • We don't run ads or ad campaigns through this integration.

Storage and revocation

Our Pinterest API access token is stored securely in our own private automation environment and is never shared or exposed publicly. Access can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest business account's own app permissions settings, which immediately disables the integration.
